Terminal Velocity Lab

Drop a sphere through a column of glycerine-like fluid. Change the radius, the density of the sphere or the viscosity of the fluid, time the fall between the two marks, and collect your own evidence for how terminal velocity depends on each one.

Set up the experiment

Fluid density is fixed at 1260 kg/m³. For a clean comparison, change one slider at a time.

Measurements

Stopwatch (A → B, 30 cm)0.000 s
Measured v = 0.30 m ÷ t–

Graph of your results

Run at least two drops that differ in only the variable you are plotting.

Your task

For each of the three variables, keep the other two fixed, take readings at 5 or more values, and plot a graph. Try different ways of plotting until the points fall on a straight line, then state the relationship you found and how well your evidence supports it.
